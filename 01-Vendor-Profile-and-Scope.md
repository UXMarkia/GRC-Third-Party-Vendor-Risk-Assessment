# Third-Party Vendor Risk Assessment

## Organization

**Company:** Midwest Financial Services (MFS)  
**Industry:** Financial Services  
**Approximate Employees:** 500

## Vendor Overview

**Vendor:** CloudHR Solutions  
**Service:** Cloud-based Human Resources Management System (HRMS)  
**Service Model:** Software as a Service (SaaS)  
**Primary Use:** Payroll, employee records, benefits administration, and employee onboarding  
**Users:** MFS Human Resources personnel and approximately 500 MFS employees

## Sensitive Data Access

CloudHR Solutions will process, store, or have access to sensitive MFS employee information, including:

- Employee personally identifiable information (PII)
- Social Security numbers
- Home addresses and contact information
- Salary and compensation information
- Bank account and direct deposit information
- Employee benefits information
- Tax and payroll documentation
- Employment and onboarding records
- User account and authentication information

## Vendor Criticality

**Criticality Rating:** High

**Rationale:**  
CloudHR Solutions is considered a high-criticality vendor because the platform processes and stores sensitive employee and payroll information while supporting essential HR functions. A prolonged service outage, data breach, or other significant security incident could disrupt payroll, benefits administration, onboarding, and access to employee records, resulting in operational, financial, privacy, and regulatory impacts to MFS.
